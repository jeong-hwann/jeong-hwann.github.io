## JeongHwan Jang

I am a robotics engineer focusing on legged robots, spanning mechanical design, control, and experimental validation.

My flagship project is **RUBI**, a waterproof biped robot that I developed end-to-end (mechanical design to control). I presented this work at the **Korea Defense Robot Society** conference, and I am currently preparing a journal submission to **Ocean Engineering**.

I also worked on multi-legged platforms: **mini-Pongbot** (quadruped; leg design contribution) and **LCRM** (hexapod; leg design, gait trajectory generation, and posture controller design). Through LCRM, I gained experience in academic writing and presented a **Late Breaking Results** poster at **IROS**.
